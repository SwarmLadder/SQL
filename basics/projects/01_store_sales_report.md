# Store Sales Report

**Deliverables**
- Paid revenue by order and customer, in integer cents.
- Top products by paid-order units.
- Product list showing those never purchased.

**Run:** `python tools/run_sql.py basics/projects/01_store_sales_report.sql`

**Extend**
1. Add monthly paid revenue.
2. Add category revenue ranked from highest to lowest.
3. Find customers with no paid order.

**Done when:** your queries run on a fresh database and results match the stated business definitions.
