# 01 · WHERE, comparisons, and NULL

**Learn**
- `WHERE` filters rows before grouping. Use `AND`, `OR`, `IN`, and `BETWEEN`.
- `NULL` means unknown or absent; test with `IS NULL`, not `= NULL`.

**Run:** `python tools/run_sql.py basics/lessons/01_where_null.sql`

**Practice**
1. Find orders that are pending or cancelled.
2. Find active products priced below 1000 cents.
3. List employees who have a manager.

**Check:** Remember SQL's three-valued logic: true, false, unknown.
